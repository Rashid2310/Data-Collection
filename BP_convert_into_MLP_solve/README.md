# Business Problem to Machine Learning Problem

## Step 1: Understand the Business Problem
Whenever you are given a **business problem**, the first step is to clearly identify the **core issue** and then think about it in **mathematical and analytical terms**.

---

### Example: Netflix – Customer Churn Problem

Let’s assume we are working at **Netflix**, and the business problem is **Customer Churn**.

**Churn Rate** refers to the percentage of users who stop using a service during a specific period of time.

**Example:**  
If 4% of Netflix users cancel their subscription every month, then the monthly churn rate is **4%**.

---

## Step 2: Business Objective
The main business goal is:

> **How can we reduce the number of users who leave Netflix?**

---

## Step 3: Converting the Business Problem into an ML Problem
    identatify the type of problem (Eg. Classification or Regression)
To solve this problem using **Machine Learning**, we can reframe it as:

- Predict **which users are likely to churn**
- Identify **key factors that influence churn**
- Take **preventive actions** such as personalized recommendations, discounts, or better content
