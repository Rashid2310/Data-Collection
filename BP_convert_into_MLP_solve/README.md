# Business Problem to Machine Learning Problem

## Step 1: Understand the Business Problem
Whenever you are given a **business problem**, the first step is to clearly understand the **core issue**.  
After that, try to translate the problem into **analytical and mathematical terms**.

---

### Example: Netflix – Customer Churn Problem

Assume we are working at **Netflix**, and the business problem is **Customer Churn**.

**Customer Churn** refers to the percentage of users who stop using a service during a specific time period.

**Example:**  
If 4% of Netflix users cancel their subscription every month, then the monthly churn rate is **4%**.

---

## Step 2: Define the Business Objective
The main business goal is:

> **How can we reduce the number of users who leave Netflix?**

---

## Step 3: Convert the Business Problem into an ML Problem
First, identify the **type of Machine Learning problem**.

In this case:
- We want to **predict whether a user will churn or not**
- This becomes a **Classification problem** (Churn: Yes / No)

Using Machine Learning, we aim to:
- Predict **which users are likely to churn**
- Identify **key factors influencing churn**
- Take **preventive actions**, such as:
  - Personalized recommendations
  - Special discounts
  - Improved content strategy

---

## Step 4: Identify the Data Required
To solve the churn problem, we need data related to user behavior, such as:
- Subscription details
- Watch time and content preferences
- Login frequency
- Payment history
- Customer support interactions

These features help the ML model learn patterns related to customer churn.

---

## Summary
- Understand the business problem clearly  
- Define a measurable business objective  
- Convert it into an ML problem (Classification / Regression)  
- Identify relevant data and features
